# Artifact - as first supplied

Everything here is invented for the example.

**Studio:** Fieldhouse (an eleven-person brand and campaign studio)
**Client:** Marra Provisions - a regional specialty food brand, roughly $60M,
family-owned, second generation now running it.
**Pitched:** 2026-08-04

---

## What the studio pasted in, first attempt

> Marra Provisions - "The Long Way"
>
> Marra has spent forty years doing things the slow way while the category
> optimized itself into sameness. Every competitor now talks about
> craftsmanship. Marra is the only one who can prove it, because they never
> stopped.
>
> The idea: **The Long Way.** A campaign built entirely on the steps Marra
> refuses to skip - the twelve-hour cure, the hand-tied casing, the small
> batches that cap their own growth. Not nostalgia. A working argument for why
> the slow version tastes different.
>
> Executions: a documentary film series shot in the facility, a packaging
> callout system marking the step each product refuses to skip, in-store
> materials, and a retail-partner story kit.
>
> We'd start with the film series in Q1.

That was the whole submission.

---

## What was missing

The studio had a client, a real concept, and actual pitch content. It had **no
commercial shape at all** - no fee, no term, no model, and no statement that the
money had been left out deliberately.

`procurement-lead` and `finance-partner` were both active seats on the fitted
roster. Neither had anything to react to.

See `2-refusal-or-gate.md`.
