# The gate - what happened before any seat ran

## The refusal, as the studio saw it

> I'm going to stop before running the panel.
>
> You've given me three of the four things I need. There's a real client
> (Marra Provisions, $60M, family-owned, second generation). There's a stated
> ask - a campaign, starting with the film series in Q1. And this is the actual
> concept, not a summary of it.
>
> What's missing is any commercial shape. No fee, no term, no model - and
> `procurement-lead` and `finance-partner` are both active seats on your Marra
> roster. If I run this now, I'd be inventing what those two people react to,
> and you'd get back a decision table with thirteen verdicts in it where two of
> them are fiction. It would look exactly like the eleven real ones.
>
> That matters more than usual here, because a documentary film series is
> exactly the kind of scope where procurement sends the proposal back for
> composition. It's the objection most likely to actually cost you this pitch,
> and right now I can't test for it.
>
> Give me the fee structure - even a range, even "we're proposing a twelve-month
> retainer at roughly X with production quoted separately" - and I'll run it.
>
> If you deliberately left money out of this deck, tell me that instead. That's
> a real choice and the panel can judge it.

## What the studio said back

> Fair. It's a 12-month retainer at $28k/month covering strategy, creative, and
> campaign management. Film production is quoted separately per film, roughly
> $40-60k each, four films in year one. We hadn't put it in the deck yet - we
> were going to talk to the fee on the call.

## The AI's response

> That last part is worth flagging before we run: "we'll talk to the fee on the
> call" is itself a decision the panel can judge, and I suspect two seats will
> have something to say about it. I'll run it as you've now described it and
> note that the deck as presented carries no numbers.
>
> Running the panel now - eleven active seats on your Marra roster.

## Roster note for this run

From `set up the committee`, run earlier for Marra:

- **Deactivated:** `comms-pr-lead` (comms is folded into marketing at Marra) and
  `incumbent-advocate` (no incumbent - Marra has used freelancers, not an
  agency).
- **Active:** 11 seats.
- **Seats that should say no:** `budget-was-never-real` and
  `builds-it-in-house` both retained. Marra has a two-person internal design
  function, so `builds-it-in-house` was edited down from a fourteen-person
  studio to a two-person team - which makes her a much softer hard-no than the
  shipped version, and that edit is recorded in `EVIDENCE-NOTES.md`.
- **Execution:** isolated-parallel, two waves.

**Notice what the refusal actually bought.** The studio was about to send a deck
with no numbers into a room containing a procurement lead. The gate surfaced
that before the panel ran, which is earlier than the panel itself would have.
