# What to notice in this example

## 1. The refusal happened before the panel, and it was the cheapest finding

The gate cost one exchange. It surfaced that a deck going into a room with a
procurement lead carried no numbers at all - which turned out to be the single
most award-threatening thing in the run, and the panel would have found it
anyway, later, more expensively.

Notice also what the AI did NOT do: it did not offer to "run it anyway assuming a
standard retainer." That would have produced eleven real verdicts and two
fabricated ones, in the same table, indistinguishable.

## 2. The verdict split does not match the sentiment

Five yes, five no, one flag - and nine of eleven seats praised the idea. If you
only read the score you would think this pitch was in trouble. It is not. Every
single NO attaches to something missing from the deck rather than something wrong
in the work.

That distinction is the main thing this kit produces. "Your idea is good and your
document is unfinished" is a completely different instruction from "your idea is
weak," and a studio reading only a 57 would get it backwards.

## 3. The hard-no did her job perfectly

`builds-it-in-house` stayed NO. She was not seduced by good work. And she named
the exact scope she WOULD advocate for - complement the internal team, do not
displace it.

That is the most reusable output in the run. Fieldhouse now knows the objection
they will face at every client with an internal creative team, and they know the
shape of the answer. They got it before the pitch instead of after the loss.

**If she had said YES here, the correct reading would be alarm, not celebration.**

## 4. The wrong-fit seat is the item to watch

`budget-was-never-real` returned INTENSITY 5, CONFIDENCE LOW, RESISTS NOTHING,
MISSING NOTHING. That combination - maximum enthusiasm with zero friction - is
the signature of someone who loves it and cannot buy it.

Here it did not trip the alarm, because two core seats matched his intensity at
higher confidence. Read example 02 next: same signal, opposite verdict.

## 5. Three seats objected to the same thing for incompatible reasons

The film series was resisted by the founder (too safe), the performance lead (too
few assets), and the CMO (too slow). One element, three unrelated objections.

A synthesis that flattened those into "concerns about the films" would have
destroyed the useful information. The fix for "too slow" is resequencing; the fix
for "too few" is an asset system; the fix for "too safe" is showing the rejected
routes. Three different actions.

## 6. What was NOT treated as a change list

`budget-was-never-real` had no objections, so there was nothing to optimize
toward - and `builds-it-in-house`'s objection was reported as diagnostic rather
than as a fix instruction. Writing a version that converts her is how a studio
ends up promising something it should not.

## 7. The arithmetic is visible on purpose

Every dimension shows raw score, the cited evidence, the weight, and the
multiplication. You can check it. A total with no visible math is a number that
carries authority it has not earned, and this kit refuses to produce one.

Note also the gate cap being stated even though it was not binding. The studio
needs to know the claim problem is not priced into the 57.
