# Artifact - as supplied

Everything here is invented for the example.

**Studio:** Nine Arrows (a six-person creative studio, strategy and design)
**Client:** Verrick Industrial Supply - a third-generation industrial
distributor, roughly $95M, 340 employees, twelve branches.
**Pitched:** 2026-08-06

The gate passed on the first submission: named client, stated ask, full deck
text, and a fee structure. Nothing was refused. See `2-refusal-or-gate.md` for
the gate record.

---

## The pitch, as submitted

> **Verrick - "The People Who Know"**
>
> Verrick competes against two national distributors with better logistics,
> deeper inventory, and lower prices. Verrick will not win on any of those and
> should stop trying.
>
> What Verrick has that neither competitor can buy: 340 people who have been
> doing this an average of eleven years, who know which fitting actually works
> in a 1970s building, and who pick up the phone. The nationals have a portal.
> Verrick has people who know the answer.
>
> **The idea: "The People Who Know."** Put the counter staff, the branch
> managers, and the field reps at the centre of everything. Not as a warm
> employer-brand gesture - as the actual competitive argument. The people ARE
> the product difference.
>
> **Executions:**
> - A named-expert directory: every branch's specialists, their actual
>   expertise, their direct line. Print, web, and on the counter.
> - "Ask the person who knows" - a campaign of real questions Verrick people
>   have answered, with the answer and the person's name.
> - Branch-level social handled by the branches themselves, with a simple kit.
> - Trade-show and counter materials built on the same idea.
>
> **The ask:** a six-month engagement at $18,000/month, all-in, no separate
> production. Six months, then we both decide.
