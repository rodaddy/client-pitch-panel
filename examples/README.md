# Worked Examples

Two complete runs, end to end: a real-shaped pitch going in, and the actual
output the kit produces coming out. Read one before your first wash so you know
what the shape looks like.

**These are illustrations, not your verify step.** The install verifies on YOUR
own pitch, with your own client's name in it, because that is the only run that
tells you whether this is useful. Read these after, as a comparison - never
instead of.

Everything in them - studios, clients, brands, people, numbers - is invented for
the example. None of it is a real engagement.

| Example | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `01-refused-then-run/` | The refusal firing on a thin pitch, the studio fixing it, and the full run that followed. This is the most important one to read. |
| `02-strong-pitch-wrong-room/` | A genuinely good pitch that the panel says stop to - because the enthusiasm is all in seats that cannot buy. |

Each folder holds:

- `1-artifact.md` - the pitch, as the studio supplied it;
- `2-refusal-or-gate.md` - what the gate did before running;
- `3-reactions.md` - the raw verdicts, seat by seat, in the ten-line contract;
- `4-synthesis-and-score.md` - the decision table, the dissent, the arithmetic,
  the integrity check, and the call;
- `NOTES.md` - what to notice in this one.

## The thing to look for in both

Not the score. Look at what the two seats built to say no actually said, and
whether the seats that loved it were the seats that can buy.
