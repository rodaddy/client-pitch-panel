# Owner Context - the studio's own side

**The seats never see this file.** It is read only during synthesis and rewrite,
after every reaction is closed. If a seat receives this, the run is compromised:
the panel would be reacting to what the studio wants rather than to the pitch.

Fill this in once, during `set up the committee`.

- Client:

- The fee or term shape you are proposing:
- Who from your team is in the room, and who would be on the account:

## What you believe

- Why you think you should win this:
- The objection you are most afraid of:
- What you would change if you could:
- Would you rather win this smaller than not at all, and where is the floor?

That last question matters more than it looks. Several seats on this panel push
toward scope reduction rather than rejection - `finance-partner` phases,
`incumbent-advocate` carves out, `procurement-lead` restructures. Knowing the
studio's floor lets the synthesis tell the difference between "this is failing"
and "this is heading toward a smaller version you would still take."

invite," which is a different and more useful question.
