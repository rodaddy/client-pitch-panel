# owner

The studio's own side of the pitch, kept in its own folder because of who is not
allowed to read it.

`OWNER-CONTEXT.md` holds the studio name, what the studio does best, what it is
genuinely not good at, who is really available for the account, and the facts of
this pitch. It is filled in once during `set up the committee`.

**The seats never see it.** It is read only during synthesis and rewrite, after
every reaction is closed. A seat that receives it compromises the run, because
the panel would then be reacting to what the studio wants rather than to the
pitch.
