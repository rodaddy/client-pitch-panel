# references

The rules the workflows apply. A workflow says what to do next; these four files
say what the rule actually is, so the rule lives in one place instead of being
restated in every workflow that needs it.

- `refusal-rules.md` - the four non-negotiables and the whole refusal rule. Runs
  before any seat, on every wash, with no exceptions.
- `seat-protocol.md` - the text that goes into a seat's task packet alongside one
  dossier, the panel objective, that seat's decision signal, and the artifact.
- `synthesis-and-scoring.md` - the separation rule (seats close before synthesis
  begins), the weighted arithmetic, and the integrity checks.
- `committee-standard.md` - what makes the roster an instrument rather than a
  cast of characters. Read before editing a seat or writing a new one.
